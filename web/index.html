Pipeline

How Taver works

Four layers, one engine — designed for edge deployment in apps, kiosks, and classrooms.

Capture & normalize

Microphone or WAV input resampled to 16 kHz mono. Lock-free queues, pre-allocated buffers — zero heap on the hot path.

Acoustic model

wav2vec2 phoneme CTC model (392 IPA symbols) runs via ONNX Runtime. Frame-level log-probabilities per sound.

Forced alignment + GOP

CTC Viterbi aligns frames to your target phoneme sequence. Peak-evidence scoring with equivalence classes for natural variation.

Why it matters

A coach that catches what STT misses

Teacher-style feedback

Per-phoneme verdicts: correct, weak, wrong sound, or swallowed — with scores and timing.

  • “You said /t/ but we need /θ/ at the start of three.”
  • Word-level roll-up for sentences and lessons.
  • Prosody: fluency, rate, pitch.

Real value for language learning

STT apps optimize for which word you said. Taver optimizes for how you said it — the gap L2 learners actually need closed.

Minimal pairs, consonant clarity, and connected speech — the skills a human tutor checks first.
